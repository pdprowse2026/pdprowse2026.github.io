P. D. Prowse was born and raised in Newfoundland, where she was raised by her maternal grandparents — two amazing people who gave her a happy childhood.

She graduated from the College of the North Atlantic in 2003 with a Business Diploma in Marketing, and left Newfoundland in 2003 for a job opportunity. She now resides in Saint John, New Brunswick with her fiancé, Brendon, and her fur babies, Riggs and Baby Kitty.

She has always had a passion for writing — scribbling stories by hand and jotting ideas down on anything she could find. But it wasn't until COVID that her vision to write a novel truly came into view.

Reflections of a Rocking Chair came to her in a dream. When she told a friend about it, he told her she needed to turn it into a story — and after several years of writing, rewriting, and changing directions, the first book of the Reflections series was born. The second book came easily; the third was the one that truly excited her; and the fifth remains her favorite.

Meet the Characters Reflections of a Rocking Chair

Main cast

  • Jael Foster — The heart of the story, telling what happened to her from the witness stand.
  • Michael — Jael's husband. Her college tutor first, the love of her life ever since.
  • Virginia "Ginny" Mackay — Jael's late mother, whose love outlasted death itself.
  • Evelyn Steeves — The trauma social worker who walks beside Jael.
  • Officer Emily Carter — The police officer on Jael's case.

Family

  • Diane — Jael's maternal grandmother.
  • Roy — Jael's maternal grandfather.
  • Jonathan — Jael's father.
  • Whitney — Jonathan's wife; Jael's stepmother.
  • Pamela — Jael's half-sister; mother of Angus and Phoenix.
  • Breela — Jael's half-sister, engaged to Chris.
  • Angus — Pamela's young son; Jael's nephew.
  • Phoenix — Pamela's young son; Jael's nephew.
  • Gloria — Jael's aunt.
  • Glen — Jael's uncle.
  • Chris — Breela's fiancé.

The trial

  • Stan Dubay — Jael's stepfather; the defendant.
  • Justice Vonnie Newburgh — The judge presiding over the trial.
  • Dr. Hayes — A clinical psychologist specializing in trauma.
  • Dr. Michael Reynolds — The emergency physician who testifies at the trial.
  • Daniel Whitmore — A private investigator hired decades ago to find a missing mother and child.
  • Cody Falcone — A plumber called as a witness at the trial.
